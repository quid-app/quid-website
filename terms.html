What Quid is

Quid is a free companion app helping international students settle into life in the UK. It offers self-managed budgeting tools, a community feed, curated student deals, first-week checklists, and Quid AI, an in-app assistant. Quid is not a bank, does not connect to your bank accounts, and does not move or hold money.
